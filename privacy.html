Cursed Strikes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cursed Strikes is a free shooting game. We keep it simple: we collect as little as possible, we show no ads, and we sell nothing.

What stays on your device

Your player name, coins, Hexbux, skins, weapons and level progress are saved on your own device (in the browser or app storage). We do not receive this information unless you sign in (see below). Clearing the app's data or pressing "Reset Progress" deletes it.

Optional account sign-in

When account sign-in is available, it is optional. If you choose to sign in with Google or email, we store your account ID, email address and your game progress (name, coins, Hexbux, skins, weapons, levels) with Google Firebase so your progress follows you between devices. We never see or store your password. You can stop signing in at any time and play without an account.

Online arena

If you play the online arena, players connect directly to each other. Your chosen player name, your position and your game actions are shared with the other players in that room while you play. Connecting directly can reveal your network (IP) address to the players in your room, and a connection helper service (PeerJS) is used to introduce players to each other. We do not keep chat or recordings, and the arena has no text chat.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No real-money purchases. Coins and Hexbux are only earned by playing.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for marketing.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above, and only when a child (with a parent's help) chooses to sign in.

Third-party services

The game loads code libraries from public content delivery networks (jsDelivr and Google). Sign-in and cloud saves use Google Firebase. These services receive standard connection information (such as an IP address) when they deliver files to you.

Children

Children should ask a parent or guardian before signing in or playing online. A parent can ask us to delete a child's account data at any time using the contact below.

Deleting your data

To delete data stored on your device, clear the app's data. To delete an account and its cloud-saved progress, contact us and we will remove it.
